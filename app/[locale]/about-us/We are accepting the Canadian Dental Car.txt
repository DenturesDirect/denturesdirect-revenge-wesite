# Denture Clinic in Toronto
Looking to repair a loose or hard-to-fit denture? Or searching for a warm, inviting, and state-of-the-art denture clinic in Toronto? Dentures Direct is here to help.

# Non-Removable Dentures / All-on-4
Our permanent, non-removable dentures are top quality and anchored by dental implants for superior stability. They don’t need to be taken out at night and can be cared for just like your natural teeth.

# Implant-Retained Dentures
Our implant-retained dentures are designed and fabricated on-site to eliminate movement. While they should still be removed at night, they securely attach to dental implants, preventing them from slipping or falling out for much higher patient satisfaction.

# Removable Partial Dentures
Our high-quality removable partial dentures are made on-site to replace one or several missing teeth. They are an affordable and minimally invasive option that restores your smile and function without the need for surgery.

# Full/Complete Dentures
Our full/complete dentures are designed and fabricated entirely on-site. They are the most affordable option to restore a full mouth of teeth and are also an excellent choice for patients who are not suitable for dental implants. With this simple solution, you can eat, chew, talk, and smile with confidence.

# Emergency Denture Repairs
When denture emergencies happen, we offer convenient, quick, and affordable repair services. We will work hard to restore the function and aesthetics of your dentures while you wait, with many repairs completed the same day.
